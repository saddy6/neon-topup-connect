# Digital Direct Hub

Design a premium, modern, and highly professional website for selling game top-ups, Google Play gift cards, and digital subscriptions. The website should feel trustworthy, clean, and appealing to gamers and digital service users.

Design Style:

Dark mode theme

Elegant neon accents (electric blue, purple, and soft neon green)

Smooth animations and modern typography

Clean layout with excellent spacing

Fully responsive and mobile-optimized

Critical Image Requirement:

Every single product must have its own unique, high-quality, realistic image.

Do not reuse or repeat the same image across different products.

Each game, each Google Play card, and each subscription must have a distinct visual that clearly represents that specific product.

Important Rules:

Do not display any prices anywhere on the website.

All purchases and communication must go through WhatsApp only.

Use the WhatsApp number: +21628415739

Every “Order Now” or “Contact via WhatsApp” button must open a direct WhatsApp chat with this number.

Place prominent WhatsApp buttons in the header, on every product card, and in the footer.

Website Structure:

Homepage

Strong hero section with a clear headline such as: “Instant Digital Top-Ups & Premium Subscriptions”

Large WhatsApp call-to-action button

Quick overview of three main categories: Game Top-Ups, Google Play Cards, and Subscriptions

“Why Choose Us” section (Instant delivery, Fast support, Guaranteed quality, Easy ordering)

Featured products section — each product must show its own unique image

Customer reviews section

Professional footer with quick links and WhatsApp button

Game Top-Ups Page

Display attractive product cards. Each game must have its own unique high-quality image:

PUBG Mobile

Free Fire

Mobile Legends

Roblox

Fortnite

Call of Duty

Genshin Impact

Valorant

Steam

Every card includes the unique game image + a clear “Order via WhatsApp” button.

Google Play Cards Page

Showcase different Google Play gift cards. Each card variation must have its own distinct high-quality image and a WhatsApp order button.

Subscriptions Page

Display premium subscription cards. Each service must have its own unique official-style image:

Netflix

Spotify

YouTube Premium

Disney+

Amazon Prime

ChatGPT Plus

Other popular digital subscriptions

Each subscription has its own distinct image and WhatsApp button.

How to Order Page

Simple 4-step process:

Choose your product

Click the WhatsApp button

Send your request

Receive it instantly

Support / Contact Page

Clear contact information with a large WhatsApp button and a simple contact form (optional).

Additional Requirements:

High-quality, realistic, and unique images for every individual product (no repeated visuals)

Large, easy-to-tap buttons optimized for mobile

Trust elements (Instant delivery badge, 24/7 support, Guaranteed service)

Clean, uncluttered design with strong visual hierarchy

Fixed header with navigation menu + 21628415739 WhatsApp button

Make the website look like a premium digital store — trustworthy, modern, and conversion-focused, encouraging visitors to order instantly through WhatsApp. Every product must feel visually distinct.
